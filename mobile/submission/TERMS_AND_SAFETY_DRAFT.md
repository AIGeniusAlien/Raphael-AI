# Terms and medical-safety draft — owner/legal review required

This development application provides a synthetic testing interface to Raphael's existing record, communication, consent and medical education/decision-support workflows. It is not an emergency service, hospital dispatch system, verified clinician identity service or independently validated medical device.

- Use only authorized synthetic accounts and records. Do not impersonate another patient, clinician or caregiver. A claimed clinician title alone grants no access.
- AI text is general education or a provisional draft. It does not constitute a diagnosis, prescription, medical order, signed clinical note or independently verified treatment recommendation. Supporting source IDs do not prove every assertion or its applicability to a particular age/population.
- A qualified participating clinician must review consequential output and explicitly approve any permitted clinical publication. No automatic signoff, medication administration, billing or real clinical delivery is authorized by this app.
- Do not delay contacting local emergency services or seeking immediate care to prepare intake, use AI or send a message. No immediate clinical response or facility monitoring is promised.
- Source excerpts and dates are inspectable. Source coverage is bounded; older dates, conflicting guidance, missing findings and model errors must be handled by appropriate clinical review. Government links do not imply government endorsement.
- Record sharing has explicit scope and expiration. Revocation blocks future authorized access where implemented, but cannot recall earlier disclosures. It does not automatically erase approved clinical documentation or audit history.
- Keep devices secure. No offline record or native microphone capability is enabled in this increment. Loss, screenshots and operating-system behavior require a separate assessed deployment policy.
- Support contact, operating entity, permitted jurisdictions, eligibility/guardian rules, dispute terms, retention and account-deletion policy remain owner/legal decisions. The source project does not supply those approvals.

These terms are not an approved legal agreement. Do not publish them unchanged or represent them as regulatory clearance.
